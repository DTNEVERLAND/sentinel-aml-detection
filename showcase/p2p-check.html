Check the buyer before the bank checks you.

If you sell crypto on P2P to a mule account, the dirty money landing in your bank can get your own account frozen when it's reported. Paste the buyer's public listing numbers below — this tells you if they fit the mule pattern. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

ENTER THE BUYER'S NUMBERS

HOW TO USE THIS
  1. On Binance / OKX / any P2P app, open the buyer's ad and tap their name to see their profile.
  2. Copy these five numbers into the boxes above:
    • Advertised price & market mid — their price vs. the going rate.
    • Account age — how long they've been registered (days).
    • Orders in 30 days — their 30-day order count.
    • Completion rate — their completion %.
  3. Read the verdict. SAFE = no mule fingerprint. CAUTION = skip them, pick another — it's not worth a frozen account for a few cents. DO NOT TRADE = strong match, walk away.
Even on SAFE, always keep the trade inside the platform's escrow and never release coins outside the app. A patient mule on an old account priced at market can still look clean on public data — escrow is what protects you when the numbers can't.

Thresholds are calibrated from real Binance P2P market data and red-tested. This is a risk signal, not legal or financial advice.
